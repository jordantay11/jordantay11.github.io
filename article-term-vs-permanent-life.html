Term vs Permanent Life Insurance

A simple guide to term and permanent life insurance.

Term life insurance

Term life insurance provides coverage for a set period, such as 10, 20, or 30 years. It is often used for temporary needs like raising children, paying a mortgage, or replacing income during working years.

Permanent life insurance

Permanent life insurance is designed to last longer, potentially for life, as long as requirements are met. It may build cash value depending on the policy type and structure.

Which one is better?

Neither is automatically better. Term insurance may be cost-effective for large temporary needs. Permanent insurance may be considered for long-term legacy, estate liquidity, business planning, or final expense goals.

How to decide

Consider your budget, age, health, dependents, debts, income, long-term goals, and how long the protection is needed.
