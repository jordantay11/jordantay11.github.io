What final expense coverage can help with

The goal is simple: reduce the financial burden placed on children, a spouse, or other loved ones during an already difficult time.

Funeral & Burial

Help provide funds for funeral home services, burial, cremation, memorial costs, or related arrangements.

Remaining Bills

Proceeds may help beneficiaries handle smaller debts, medical balances, travel, or other immediate expenses.

Family Breathing Room

A planned death benefit can be more dependable than expecting relatives to fund expenses out of pocket or rely on fundraising.

Is final expense different from life insurance?

Final expense is life insurance. The distinction is primarily the purpose, coverage amount, underwriting approach, and target need. It is commonly structured as permanent coverage.

Need more than final expenses?

If you also want to replace income, protect a mortgage, leave an inheritance, or cover larger obligations, a broader life-insurance plan may be more appropriate.

Common questions

Clear answers before you make a decision.

Who is final expense insurance for?

It is often considered by people who want a modest death benefit specifically earmarked in their planning for funeral and other end-of-life expenses.

Is there a medical exam?

Underwriting varies by carrier and product. Some policies use health questions and records rather than a traditional exam; eligibility and pricing depend on the specific policy.

How much coverage should I consider?

Consider expected funeral or cremation costs, remaining bills, other immediate obligations, and any money already set aside for those needs.
