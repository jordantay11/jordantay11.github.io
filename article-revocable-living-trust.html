Benefits of a Revocable Living Trust

Learn the major benefits and limitations of revocable living trusts.

What is a revocable living trust?

A revocable living trust is a legal arrangement that can hold assets during your lifetime and provide instructions for management if you become incapacitated or pass away. “Revocable” means it can generally be changed or canceled while you are living and competent.

Major benefits

  • Probate avoidance: Properly funded trust assets may avoid probate.
  • Privacy: Trust administration may be more private than court probate.
  • Incapacity planning: A successor trustee can help manage trust assets if you cannot.
  • Organization: A trust helps centralize instructions for assets and beneficiaries.

What a trust does not automatically do

A trust must usually be funded to work as intended. That means assets need to be retitled or beneficiary designations coordinated. A trust is also not a substitute for insurance, tax planning, or long-term care planning.
