Medicare and Long-Term Care Myths

Understand why Medicare may not cover the care families expect.

The common myth

Many people believe Medicare will pay for long-term care. Medicare may cover some skilled care under specific conditions, but it generally does not pay for ongoing custodial care such as help with bathing, dressing, or daily supervision.

Why this matters

If a family waits until care is needed, the available options may be limited. Planning earlier can help preserve choices and reduce stress.

What to review

  • Your retirement income and savings
  • Family caregiving availability
  • Insurance options
  • Home safety and aging-in-place needs
  • Legal and healthcare decision documents
