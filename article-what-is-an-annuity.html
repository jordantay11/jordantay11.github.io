What Is an Annuity?

A plain-English explanation of annuities and retirement income planning.

An annuity in simple terms

An annuity is a contract with an insurance company. Depending on the type, it may help accumulate money, protect principal, or create scheduled income.

Why retirees consider annuities

  • To create predictable income
  • To reduce the risk of outliving assets
  • To add stability alongside market-based investments
  • To plan for a spouse or beneficiary

Important tradeoffs

Annuities can include fees, surrender charges, tax rules, and limitations on access. The details matter, so contracts should be reviewed carefully.

Where annuities may fit

Annuities may be used alongside Social Security, pensions, retirement accounts, savings, and investment portfolios as part of a broader retirement income strategy.
