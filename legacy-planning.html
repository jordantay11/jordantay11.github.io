Legacy Planning

Learn how legacy planning goes beyond documents to include values, wishes, family guidance, and protection strategies.

What is legacy planning?

Legacy planning is the broader process of deciding how you want your values, assets, wishes, and responsibilities carried forward. It often includes estate planning documents, beneficiary designations, insurance, family conversations, and written guidance for loved ones.

Why it matters

A strong legacy plan helps loved ones understand what you wanted and why. It can reduce conflict, provide clarity, and make sure important details are not lost during a crisis.

Key benefits

  • Clarity: Loved ones know who to contact, where documents are kept, and what priorities matter.
  • Family protection: Planning can help preserve assets and reduce unnecessary confusion.
  • Values transfer: You can share the principles, stories, and intentions behind your decisions.
  • Coordination: Align trusts, wills, beneficiary designations, insurance, and long-term care plans.

What to include

A legacy plan may include a family information organizer, contact list, passwords or access instructions, beneficiary review, funeral wishes, charitable goals, business succession notes, and guidance for children or heirs.
