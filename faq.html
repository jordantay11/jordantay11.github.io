Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common planning questions.

What is the difference between a will and a trust?

A will directs wishes after death and may go through probate. A trust may help manage and transfer assets privately and efficiently when properly created and funded.


Do I need life insurance if I have a trust?

Possibly. Trusts and life insurance solve different problems. Insurance can create liquidity for family needs, debts, education, and final expenses.


What is long-term care planning?

Planning for possible future care needs such as home care, assisted living, or nursing care.


Do you provide legal advice?

No. This site is for education and planning guidance. Legal documents and legal advice should be reviewed with a licensed attorney.
