Understanding Long-Term Care Costs

Learn why long-term care can become one of the biggest retirement risks.

Long-term care is a family issue

Care needs can affect more than the person receiving care. Spouses, adult children, and relatives may need to provide time, transportation, money, or coordination.

Common types of care

  • Home care
  • Adult day services
  • Assisted living
  • Memory care
  • Nursing home care

Why costs can be difficult

Care can last months or years. Even moderate monthly expenses can quickly affect retirement savings and family cash flow.

Planning options

Families may discuss long-term care insurance, hybrid life insurance with long-term care benefits, savings strategies, home modifications, family caregiving plans, and legal planning.
