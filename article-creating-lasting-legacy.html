Creating a Lasting Legacy

Legacy planning goes beyond money and documents.

Legacy is more than assets

A lasting legacy includes values, stories, family guidance, charitable goals, and the way loved ones are cared for after you are gone.

Practical steps

  • Write down important contacts and document locations.
  • Review beneficiaries on accounts and policies.
  • Discuss wishes with trusted family members.
  • Create a plan for incapacity and healthcare decisions.
  • Consider how insurance, trusts, and long-term care planning work together.

Why conversations matter

Documents are important, but family understanding is also powerful. A clear conversation can reduce conflict and help loved ones honor your intentions.
